# 📊 Business Case Studies

> Business and operational case studies focused on transforming data, metrics, and business context into actionable insights and better decisions.

---

## 🎯 About This Repository

This repository contains business case studies developed to explore how analytical thinking can support operational and strategic decision-making.

The studies focus on connecting business questions with:

* data analysis
* KPIs and business metrics
* customer behavior
* operational performance
* sales and marketing results
* reporting
* business strategy
* data-driven decision-making

The objective is not only to calculate metrics, but to understand what those metrics reveal about business performance and what actions they can support.

---

## 🔎 Analytical Approach

Each case study follows a business-oriented analytical process:

```text
BUSINESS PROBLEM
       ↓
BUSINESS QUESTIONS
       ↓
DATA AND CONTEXT
       ↓
METRICS AND KPIs
       ↓
ANALYSIS
       ↓
INSIGHTS
       ↓
RECOMMENDATIONS
       ↓
BUSINESS DECISION
```

The central idea is simple:

> Analytics becomes valuable when it helps answer a relevant business question.

## 🧠 From Metric to Insight

A number alone does not explain a business.

For example:

```text
Sales decreased by 12%
        ↓
What changed?
        ↓
Which products?
Which customers?
Which period?
Which channel?
        ↓
What business factors may explain it?
        ↓
What action should be considered?
```

This repository focuses on moving beyond descriptive reporting and using analytical reasoning to understand the business context behind the numbers.

---

## 📊 Case Study Structure

Whenever applicable, the case studies in this repository follow this structure:

### 1. Business Context

Description of the scenario and the business environment.

### 2. Problem

Definition of the issue or opportunity being investigated.

### 3. Business Questions

Questions the analysis should help answer.

### 4. Metrics and KPIs

Identification of the indicators relevant to the problem.

### 5. Analysis

Evaluation of available information, patterns, trends, and relationships.

### 6. Insights

Main findings generated by the analysis.

### 7. Recommendations

Possible actions based on the evidence identified.

### 8. Expected Business Impact

How the recommendation could influence performance, efficiency, customer experience, risk, or decision-making.
